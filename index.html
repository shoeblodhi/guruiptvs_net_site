Coming up

The half of the year
the sofa fills up

Cricket tours & domestic seasons

Test and limited-overs tours run straight through the second half of the year, and the domestic T20 calendar follows. Kick-off times land badly in North America — which is exactly what catch-up is for.

The new football year

European league football restarts in August and runs to May. Between the early kick-offs and the late ones, there is football on a North American screen most weekends from now until spring.

NFL and NBA seasons

American football returns in September and basketball in October. If your household splits between desi TV and US sport, that is one subscription covering both arguments.

Festival programming

Onam, Durga Puja, Navratri, Diwali — the specials, the music nights and the film premieres that run on Indian channels and never make it onto a North American streaming service.

Straight answers

Guru IPTV, questioned

How much does Guru IPTV cost in the USA and Canada?

Guru IPTV is sold in fixed terms rather than monthly billing: $79 for 6 months, $120 for 1 year, $220 for 3 years and $330 for 5 years, in USD. Every term includes the same service — 15,000+ live channels, 100,000+ on-demand titles, 4K where the source supports it, 7-day catch-up and 24/7 support. The longer terms simply lower the effective monthly cost, down to about $5.50 a month on the 5-year plan.

Is Guru IPTV legal to use?

Watching television over the internet is legal in both the USA and Canada — IPTV is just a delivery method, the same one used by mainstream carriers. What matters legally is whether the channels being delivered are properly licensed for your territory. Ask any provider, including us, which channels they are licensed to carry in your country before you subscribe, and keep the answer. If a provider will not answer that question, that is your answer.

What is on the Guru IPTV channel list?

The line-up spans 15,000+ live channels across Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, Urdu and English, covering general entertainment, news, movies, music, kids, devotional and sport — including cricket, kabaddi and football. On-demand adds 100,000+ films and series. The list moves as feeds change, so we send the current channel list for your language mix on request rather than publishing a snapshot that goes stale.

Do I need a Guru IPTV box, or will my Firestick work?

Your Firestick will work. So will a Samsung or LG Smart TV, an Android TV box, Apple TV, an iPhone or iPad, an Android phone or tablet, and a Windows or Mac computer. A dedicated set-top box is optional — most people take it only if they want a single device that boots straight into live TV without navigating a smart TV home screen first.

How do I buy a Guru IPTV subscription?

Message us on WhatsApp or call, tell us which languages your household actually watches and which device you want it on, and pick a term. We confirm what your line-up will look like, take payment, and walk you through the install on the same thread. Most first-time setups are finished inside one conversation.

Can I watch live cricket and the new football season on Guru IPTV?

Live sport is part of the service: cricket tours and domestic T20, kabaddi, and league football, alongside general sports channels. Because so much of it starts in the middle of the North American night, the 7-day catch-up window matters more than the live feed for most households — you can start the match at breakfast and avoid the score all morning.

How many devices can watch at the same time?

Multi-device streaming is included, and the number of simultaneous streams depends on how your line is provisioned. Tell us how many screens run at once in your house — typically a living room TV plus one or two phones or tablets — and we will set the connection count to match before you pay, so nobody gets locked out mid-match.

What internet speed do I need for 4K?

Roughly 15–25 Mbps per stream is comfortable for HD, and 25 Mbps or more for 4K. Stability matters more than headline speed: a steady 30 Mbps connection outperforms a 300 Mbps one that drops packets. If you are on Wi-Fi at the far end of the house, a wired connection or a mesh node near the TV usually fixes buffering faster than upgrading your plan.

Does Guru IPTV work across Canada as well as the USA?

Yes — the service is set up for households across all 50 US states and every Canadian province, including the Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga, Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Montreal and Winnipeg areas where the South Asian community is largest. Support runs on North American hours as well as overnight, so a Vancouver evening and a New Jersey morning both get a live answer.

Is there a free trial before I pay?

Yes. Ask for a trial on WhatsApp and we will set one up on your own device so you can check the channels you care about, at your normal evening internet speed, before any money changes hands. Test the specific channels your family watches — a trial that only proves the popular ones work is not much of a trial.
